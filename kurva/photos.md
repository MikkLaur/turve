# Kurva Nokitseb: photo record

Generated by `tools/photos_md.py` from `tools/crops.txt` and the last run (`work/<id>.json`). Sources are in
`reklaamikampaania/.../kurvanokitseb.ee/previous-kurva-work/`. All processing is local; outputs are in `site/img/`
(git-ignored) as AVIF and WebP at the widths listed, exactly 3:4 (height = round(width x 4/3)), metadata stripped.

**Straightening method.** darktable's automatic line detection only runs in its GUI, so `darktable-cli` and XMP
sidecars were not used. `tools/pipeline.py` detects long lines (Canny + probabilistic Hough, > 15 % of the height),
fits a rotation and a vertical keystone to the near-vertical ones (coarse-to-fine), warps and crops to 3:4. Rotation is
capped at 4 degrees and the keystone at 0.08 to 0.12, because a full correction of steep wide-angle phone shots
shears marble, cabinets and door frames. Angles are the median lean of long near-vertical lines, in degrees
(only where enough lines were found; "n/a" otherwise). **Not every photo is squared to within 0.5 degrees** (see the
status column): that is the open question for the owner.

## Grade

- After and Kristo: white balance from neutral highlights (limited to +-10 %; off for LED-lit scenes), a warm tint that fades
  out in the shadows, lifted dark mids with black held, CLAHE local contrast at 18 % (none in the blacks), vibrance
  on mid-chroma colour only so white marble stays white.
- Before, during, process: levels, `-colorspace LinearGray`, `-sigmoidal-contrast 3x50%`, multiply with `#e4ded3`.
